Services

Sales support for every stage of your Japan entry

From checking whether the market wants your product to keeping the pipeline full, pick the stage you are at and we will build the scope around it.

  1. Home
  2. Services
Step 01

Test marketing

Before you open an office or hire anyone, find out whether Japanese buyers actually want your product. We run a short, focused outbound campaign and report back with real responses.

  • Target list built around your ideal customer
  • Pitch and talk script adapted for Japanese buyers
  • Response data by industry, company size and job title
  • A clear recommendation on whether and how to scale
Step 02

Trade shows & lead follow-up

Japanese trade shows are a great place to meet buyers, but most leads go cold once your team flies home. We call every lead while interest is still warm and turn business cards into scheduled meetings.

  • Booth support at live events on request
  • Prioritizing leads by how warm they are
  • Follow-up calls in Japanese within days, not weeks
  • Long-term nurturing for “not yet” leads
Step 03

Telemarketing & inside sales

In Japanese B2B, the phone is still one of the most reliable ways to reach decision makers. We handle the whole front end of sales, from list screening to calls and CRM logging.

  • List screening and cleanup
  • Getting past reception and reaching the right person
  • Nurturing inbound, trade show and download leads
  • Every call logged in your CRM or shared report
Step 04

Ongoing new business development

Once the market responds, we keep the pipeline full. Scripts, lists and targeting keep improving based on what actually converts, reviewed with you every week.

  • Sales strategy and hypothesis building
  • Appointment setting and meeting creation
  • Weekly reviews and daily activity logs
  • Know-how that stays inside your company
For your industry

Programs built around specific industries

HR & recruitment services

Reaching hiring companies and HR managers for recruitment agencies, staffing firms and hiring platforms, from first contact to lead management.

Corporate training & reskilling

Approaching HR departments, training managers and executives for corporate training and talent development services.

Local governments & public sector

Finding the right department and contact at local governments and public institutions, and creating proposal opportunities with the careful communication the sector expects.

PR & media outreach, as part of sales

When Japanese buyers want third-party credibility before a meeting, we support media outreach alongside your sales activity. It is not a separate product, just one more way to help you win meetings.
